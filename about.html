About Grief Portrait

Computation as memorial

Concept

Half of the world's 7,000 languages may vanish by 2100. Most will leave no audio recording, no dictionary, no trace. Grief Portrait asks: what if a language's final presence could be visual?

Each generative form grows from the phonetic structure of the language itself — Unicode values map to color, speaker count to density, vitality to saturation. The result is not a representation of the language, but a portrait of its absence.

Method

Built with p5.js and Perlin noise flow fields. Each particle's hue derives from the language's name in its own script. Fewer particles for dying languages. Slower movement for contemplation. The code is the memorial.
